# Human Usability and Comprehension Protocol

## Participants
15–20 non-technical participants across general users, families, students, and small businesses.

## Tasks
1. Check a suspicious link.
2. Explain “insufficient data” in their own words.
3. Analyze an urgent transfer message.
4. Find emergency actions after entering a password.
5. Delete local history.

## Success criteria
- At least 80% correctly interpret the verdict and uncertainty.
- At least 80% find the primary safe action.
- No participant interprets DNS resolution or a green visual as a safety guarantee.
- Consent and local-storage behavior are understood.

Do not use live malicious links or collect credentials, OTPs, financial data, or unredacted private messages.
