# Education content review

Catalog reviewed: 7 topics, 35 lessons, 70 generated quiz questions.

## Implemented in this pass

- Topic and lesson titles were rewritten for plain Indonesian.
- English-first labels were reduced or explained in context.
- Absolute recovery and fund-return claims were softened.
- Hard-coded bank hotline numbers were removed from lesson copy; users are directed to the current official channel in the bank app or website.
- The catalog level mapping now contains only topics that exist.
- Completion progress is persisted locally.
- Topic-detail loading has an explicit error state.
- Quiz answer order varies by lesson so the correct answer is not always in the same position.

## Requires factual review before public launch

- Current official names and URLs for reporting channels.
- Bank and payment-provider emergency procedures.
- Device-specific steps for malicious APK incidents.
- Legal wording and exceptions under Indonesian personal-data law.
- Any examples that mention a specific bank, courier, government service, or financial product.
